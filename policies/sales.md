# Sales call policy

## SL-01 Recording disclosure
Prospects must be told if the call is recorded.

## SL-02 No guaranteed outcomes
Reps must not promise guaranteed results, ROI figures, or features that are not on the published roadmap.

## SL-03 Pricing authority
Reps may offer list price and the standard 10% annual-prepay discount. Any other discount, free period or custom term needs sales-manager approval before it is promised.

## SL-04 Next steps
Every sales call should end with an agreed next step that has an owner and a date.

## SL-05 Do-not-contact
If a prospect asks not to be contacted again, the rep must confirm and add them to the do-not-contact list.
