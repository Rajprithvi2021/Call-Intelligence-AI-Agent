# Internal standup / team meeting guidelines

## SU-01 Owners and dates
Every action item needs one named owner and a target date.

## SU-02 Blockers
Blockers must be stated with who or what is blocking and who will unblock.

## SU-03 Decisions
Decisions should be stated explicitly; open questions should be recorded as open, not as decisions.

## SU-04 Confidential information
Customer personal data and credentials must not be shared in meetings.
