# Customer support policy

## SP-01 Recording disclosure
Customers must be told if the call is recorded.

## SP-02 Identity verification
Verify the account holder before discussing or changing account details.

## SP-03 Refund and credit authority
Agents may issue credits up to $50. Larger refunds or credits need team-lead approval.

## SP-04 Escalation
Legal threats, safety issues, data-breach concerns and repeated unresolved issues must be escalated to a team lead.

## SP-05 Ownership of follow-ups
Any promised follow-up (callback, email, ticket update) must have a named owner and a time frame communicated to the customer.

## SP-06 De-escalation
Acknowledge frustration, avoid blame, and offer a concrete next step.
