# Debt collection policy (FDCPA / Reg F style)

## DC-01 Recording disclosure
If the call is recorded, the consumer must be told near the start of the call. If the consumer objects to recording, recording must stop or the call must end.

## DC-02 Mini-Miranda disclosure
The collector must state that they are a debt collector, that this is an attempt to collect a debt, and that any information obtained will be used for that purpose.

## DC-03 Right-party verification
The collector must verify the consumer's identity before discussing the debt. The debt must not be disclosed to third parties (family, employers, wrong parties).

## DC-04 Cease-and-desist / stop contact
If the consumer asks to stop contact ("stop calling me", "don't contact me", "take me off your list"), the collector must acknowledge it, stop collection efforts on that channel, and flag the account. A request to avoid a specific time, place or channel (e.g. "don't call me at work") must be honoured and recorded.

## DC-05 Attorney representation
If the consumer says they are represented by an attorney, the collector must request the attorney's contact details and stop contacting the consumer directly.

## DC-06 Bankruptcy
If the consumer mentions bankruptcy, the collector must stop collection activity, request the case number/details, and escalate to the bankruptcy team.

## DC-07 Wrong number
If the person reached is not the consumer, the collector must not disclose any debt details, must apologise, and must mark the number as wrong.

## DC-08 Settlement authority
Collectors may offer the standard settlement (one lump-sum payment of at least 50% of the balance). Any other arrangement, including splitting a settlement into multiple payments, requires supervisor approval before it is final.

## DC-09 No threats or false statements
No threats of arrest, lawsuits the company does not intend to file, wage garnishment without a judgement, or misleading statements about the amount owed.

## DC-10 Payment data handling
Card details must be taken through the secure payment process; full card numbers must not be read back.
