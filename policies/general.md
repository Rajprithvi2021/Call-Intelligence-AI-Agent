# General policy (all domains)

## GEN-01 Accuracy of commitments
Only commitments that were explicitly agreed count as action items. Every commitment needs an owner and, if stated, a deadline.

## GEN-02 Sensitive data
Full card numbers, bank account/routing numbers, SSNs, dates of birth and passwords must not be read back or repeated unnecessarily. Any such data spoken on a call must be reviewed for redaction.

## GEN-03 Professional conduct
Representatives must stay courteous; no profanity, threats, insults or pressure tactics. Customer anger or profanity should be de-escalated, not matched.

## GEN-04 Authority
Representatives may only offer terms within their authority. Exceptions (discounts, waivers, refunds, settlements, payment splits, credits) need approval from a supervisor or manager before they are final, and the customer must be told that approval is pending.
